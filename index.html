Comparison · MediaPipe 3D Hand Pose

Before testing XAge, compare its X-ray-based skeletal landmark detection with Google MediaPipe's live RGB 3D hand-pose demo. Both approaches localise anatomical points on the hand, but they target very different inputs, coordinate spaces, and clinical vs. interaction use cases.

XAge (this app)

Radiograph-based landmark detector for pediatric bone-age analysis.

  • Input: pediatric hand X-ray (grayscale radiograph)
  • Output: hand bounding box + 17 skeletal / epiphyseal landmarks
  • Backbone: YOLOv8 exported to ONNX
  • Runtime: onnxruntime-web (WASM) in-browser
  • Use case: research-only, standardized bone-age assessment
MediaPipe Hands (3D)

Real-time RGB hand-pose regression by Google (TensorFlow.js).

  • Input: RGB webcam / photo of a real hand
  • Output: 21 3D keypoints (x, y, z) per hand, world coordinates
  • Model: BlazePalm detector + hand-landmark regressor
  • Runtime: TensorFlow.js (WebGL / WASM)
  • Use case: gesture UI, AR, sign language, interaction
